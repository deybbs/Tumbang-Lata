Tumbang Lata

Knock down the can with your tsinelas, grab it back, and run behind the line before the taya tags you. Get caught and you become the taya!

🎯 Throw

Click/touch and drag backward, then release to throw your tsinelas — like a slingshot.

🏃 Run

Move with WASD / arrow keys, or hold on the field to run toward your finger.

⚠️ Rules

The taya can only tag while the can is standing and the thrower is past the line. Knock it down and make it back = +1.

🔄 Switch roles

Tagged? You lose a life and become the TAYA. Pick up the fallen can, put it back in the circle, then tag the CPU for +2 to throw again.
